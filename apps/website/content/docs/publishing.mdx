## Before you share a recording

- Use a script and recordings you created or have permission to use for the project.
- Get the speaker’s permission to clone their voice and use the generated audio as intended, including commercial use if applicable.
- Follow your publishing platform’s rules and any disclosure requirements for synthetic audio.

Phonivra’s code uses the MIT license. Resemble AI lists the Chatterbox model repositories used by Phonivra under MIT. Permission to publish a specific narration also depends on its inputs and how you plan to use it.

## Watermark and credits

Chatterbox applies Resemble AI’s PerTh watermark during synthesis. Follow any required disclosures even when the audio has a watermark.

See the project’s [generated audio guide](https://github.com/AbhishekSinghDev/phonivra/blob/master/GENERATED_AUDIO.md) for details on publishing, credits, and the upstream terms.
