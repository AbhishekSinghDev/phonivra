---
title: Make your first narration
description: Generate a short clip with the default voice, listen to it, and save it.
---

## Add a short script

After [setup](/docs/install), open **Text to speech**. Type or paste a sentence into the script editor.

> The room went quiet. Then someone knocked.

## Choose a voice and language

For this example, set **Language** to **English** and **Voice** to **Original narrator**. You can use Multilingual V3 from setup. To change models, open **Advanced settings → Model and language detection**.

Set **Format** to **WAV**. You can choose another [audio format](/docs/audio) for your next take.

## Generate and listen

Choose **Generate narration** and wait for the result. Play the finished audio in the result panel. If a line sounds rushed, add a pause or adjust the speed and generate another take.

Phonivra saves finished takes in the audio library. Choose **Save audio** to put a copy in a folder of your choice. Choose **Details & exports** to save subtitles, the transcript, or generation metadata.
