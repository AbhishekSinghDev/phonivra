## Quick start

1. [Install Phonivra](/docs/install) and download a speech model during setup.
2. [Make a short narration](/docs/first-narration) with the default voice.
3. [Save the audio](/docs/audio) and any subtitles or transcript you need.

## Keep exploring

- [Compare speech models](/docs/models) by language and voice controls.
- [Generate chapters in a batch](/docs/batch) to get a separate audio file for each chapter.
- [Find where your work is stored](/docs/storage) and learn how to generate offline.
- [Troubleshoot setup and generation](/docs/troubleshooting) if you get stuck.
- [Check permissions before publishing](/docs/publishing) narration you plan to share or sell.
