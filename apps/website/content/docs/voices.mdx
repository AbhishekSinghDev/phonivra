## Add a voice

Open **Voices** and choose how you want to start.

- **Default voice.** Use the voice that comes with the model.
- **Import a recording.** Pick a clip with one speaker and little background noise.
- **Record a sample.** Use your microphone to capture a clear clip.

When saving an IndicF5 voice preset, use 5–12 seconds of clear speech and enter the exact words spoken. Other models can use presets without transcripts. Phonivra saves up to 40 seconds of reference audio as mono WAV at 24 kHz. Use your own voice or get the speaker’s permission. See [publishing generated audio](/docs/publishing) if you plan to share the result.

## Listen before generating

1. Select a voice preset.
2. Choose the preview model and language.
3. Generate a preview and listen to it before using the voice on a longer script.

Previewing also selects the voice for narration and batch work. Check the model and language when you return to the studio. Clear temporary previews in **Settings → Storage and downloads**.

## If a recording fails

- **Microphone missing?** Check your system input and permissions. For a source install, confirm that FFmpeg can record on your platform. You can also import a recording made elsewhere.
- **Reference rejected?** Follow the message about the recording. Try the default voice to see whether the model works on its own.
