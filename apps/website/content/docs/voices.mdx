## Add a voice

In **Voice collection**, choose **Original narrator** and **Use this voice** for the built-in Chatterbox voice. To add your own voice, choose **Create a voice**. Use **Upload audio** to import a clip or **Record** to use your microphone. Enter a name and choose **Save as voice preset**.

When saving an IndicF5 voice preset, use 5–12 seconds of clear speech and enter the exact words spoken. Other models can use presets without transcripts. Phonivra saves up to 40 seconds of reference audio as mono WAV at 24 kHz. Use your own voice or get the speaker’s permission. See [publishing generated audio](/docs/publishing) if you plan to share the result.

## Listen before generating

1. Select a voice in **Voice collection**, then open **Create a voice**. Clear any attached recording to preview the selected preset.
2. Choose the preview model and language.
3. Select **Generate voice preview** and listen before generating the full script.

Previewing also selects the voice for narration and batch work. Changes to the model and language also apply in **Text to speech** and **Batch studio**. Clear temporary previews in **Settings → Storage and downloads**.

## If a recording fails

- **Microphone missing?** Check your system input and permissions. For a source install, confirm that FFmpeg can record on your platform. You can also import a recording made elsewhere.
- **Reference rejected?** Follow the message about the recording. When using Chatterbox, try the default voice to check whether the recording is the problem.
