title: Play and export audio
description: Find finished takes, choose an audio format, and save subtitles or a transcript.
---

## Choose your audio settings

Set **Format** before generating. Choose **WAV** for editing or **MP3** for a smaller file to share. You can also export **FLAC**, **OGG**, and **M4A**.

- **Sample rate** defaults to mono audio at 24 kHz. You can also select 44.1 or 48 kHz.
- **Speed** changes the pace while preserving pitch.
- **Mastering** lets you trim silence, adjust loudness, and add fades.

Try these settings on a short take before using them on a long script.

## Find a finished take

1. Open **Library** and search for the take by title.
2. Select the take to listen, view its settings, or export it.

You can also play a new take directly in **Text to speech**. Audio placed in the output folder appears in the library and may have limited details.

## Save audio and companion files

Choose **Save audio** for a copy of the audio file. Open the take details to save available companion files individually or download a ZIP.

| File       | Contents                                                                                        |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SRT or VTT | Approximate subtitle timing based on the generated parts. Review the timing before publishing.  |
| TXT        | The script as prepared for speech, including cleanup and pronunciation changes.                 |
| JSON       | Information about the take, including the model, voice, and generation settings when available. |
| ZIP        | Audio and its available companion files.                                                        |

A project JSON and an exported metadata JSON serve different purposes. Use **Save project** in the editor when you want to reopen the editable script and settings.
